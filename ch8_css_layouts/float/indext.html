SOMALI referee rejected entry to US

In a recent incident, a Somali referee was denied entry to the United States, sparking discussions about immigration policies and the treatment of international sports officials. The referee, who has officiated numerous international matches, was reportedly stopped at the border due to visa issues. This has raised concerns among the sports community about the challenges faced by referees and officials traveling for their duties. The incident highlights the need for clearer guidelines and support for international sports professionals to ensure they can perform their roles without unnecessary obstacles.
